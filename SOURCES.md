## Deliberate simplifications

All numbers in `mission_logic.py` were invented for instructional gameplay: mass (kg), power (W), daily data units, launch capacity, budget credits, science value and scoring. The NASA references do **not** supply these specific game values. Treat these as hypothetical and do not represent as official NASA specifications. No actual launch vehicle is claimed to have the stated limits. The simulated launch is purely a rule-based narrative outcome.
